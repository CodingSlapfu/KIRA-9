# Architecture and reference provenance

Read November 6, 2026. No GPL source, generated coefficients, Japanese voice material, glottal tables, character assets or recorded voices were copied/imported into this prototype.

- Issue #2 second-pass research: https://github.com/CodingSlapfu/KIRA-9/issues/2 . Miku-FM1 pinned 88b0ef3 architecture/source and table-generator comments inspected for high-level structure only: six resonant paths, differentiated excitation, calibration and control-rate scheduling. Its GPL-3.0 implementation/data were NOT reused. Its Japanese validation does not establish English quality.
- Hillenbrand, Getty, Clark & Wheeler (1995), JASA 97, 3099–3111, DOI https://doi.org/10.1121/1.411872 (Table V). Our target frequencies are rounded aggregate female F1–F4 facts, not a recorded voicebank or a particular speaker. Paper record: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/7759650/ . Numerical table inspected at https://github.com/goiosunsw/vocalsims/blob/master/Vowel_formants_Hillenbrand_et_al_1995.csv ; primary dataset hosted with the author's permission: https://github.com/santiagobarreda/hillenbrand_et_al_1995 (MIT repository license inspected). **No audio was downloaded or imported**; no audio reuse license/consent claim is made.
- Klatt (1980), “Software for a cascade/parallel formant synthesizer,” JASA 67, 971–995. Primary paper inspected at https://www.fon.hum.uva.nl/david/ma_ssp/doc/Klatt-1980-JAS000971.pdf . General separate voiced/frication topology and English phonetic methodology informed design; no FORTRAN listings, implementation or parameter tables copied. MIT teaching reference: https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/6-542j-laboratory-on-the-physiology-acoustics-and-perception-of-speech-fall-2005/pages/labs/lab15/ .
- UNSW authors' soprano resonance research: https://www.phys.unsw.edu.au/jw/soprane.html and https://www.animations2.physics.unsw.edu.au/jw/reprints/Joliveauetal.pdf . The prototype's R1 floor and pitch calibration are **our explicit hypotheses**, not a claim that formant tuning guarantees intelligibility. No audio/examples from those pages imported.

Original code: polynomial glottal flow with pitch-independent differentiated amplitude, unit-peak TPT state-variable bandpasses, F1/F2/F3/F4/nasal/air slots, separate frication resonator and glottal-gated aspiration. Original offline calibration rule: min(8, .10/RMS, .30/peak) over the measured phone/voice/pitch cells. No Miku coefficients or control constants were transplanted. Existing KIRA MIT event scheduler, phoneme IDs, face API and bounded articulation helper are reused unchanged in an isolated adapter.
