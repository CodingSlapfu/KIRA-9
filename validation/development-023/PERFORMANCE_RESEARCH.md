# Performance study and original implementation

The reference performers inform technique categories, never voice cloning or copied melodies. No audio was downloaded or sampled. Implementation choices below are original engineering interpretations, not claims of replicating a singer.

- Michael Jackson: rhythmic vocal colour and percussive phrasing studied through the research article https://www.tandfonline.com/doi/full/10.1080/03007766.2011.618053 and his interview https://blankonblank.org/interviews/michael-jackson-godliness/ . Original pop/house/IDM contours use a bounded initial accent settling into the note, retaining exact score/MIDI timing.
- Mariah Carey: register, breath and resonant contrast described in her interview https://pitchfork.com/features/interview/forever-mariah-an-interview-with-an-icon/ . Original soul profile lets held vowels bloom and vibrato deepen; no whistle register or celebrity timbre is claimed.
- Gwen Stefani: expressive vibrato and direct delivery discussed in https://www.gq.com/story/gwen-stefani-truth-interview and https://shefani-archive.info/gwen-stefani-interviews/2019/3/3/harpers-amp-queen-december-2004 . Original pop contours emphasize attack/release contrast with a different vibrato rate from soul.
- Beth Gibbons: performance presentation https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=G_-DftINZKg and https://www.theguardian.com/music/article/2024/jun/10/beth-gibbons-review-a-transcendent-and-an-almost-otherworldly-presence . Original trip-hop delivery uses a gentle onset, slow bloom and limited airy ending. This is an interpretation of intimacy/dynamic contrast; no Gibbons recording enters the engine.
- Björk: first-person breathing/composition interview https://www.tpr.org/2017-11-20/bjork-invites-you-to-her-utopia and research https://mtosmt.org/issues/mto.08.14.4/mto.08.14.4.burns_lafrance_hawley.html . Original ethereal/experimental contours retain connected phrases and permit sustained dynamic growth without random note or syllable rewriting.

Human listening determines whether these changes are musically successful. Compile/parity/headroom tests cannot establish beauty, naturalness or extraordinary performance quality.
