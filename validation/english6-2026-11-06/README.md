## Architecture and provenance
Read Issue #2 and inspected Miku-FM1's six-band calibration, glottal excitation, separate consonant-noise and pitch-compensation architecture. No GPL implementation, coefficient table, voice data, audio or artwork imported. Original MIT prototype uses six unit-peak TPT bands (F1/F2/F3/F4/nasal/air), an original differentiated polynomial glottal pulse, separate frication/burst resonator and control-rate interpolation. Independent English aggregate formants, not Japanese vowels, supply factual targets. See experiments/english6/REFERENCES.md for Hillenbrand 1995, Klatt 1980, MIT and UNSW primary references and limitations. Source files are experiments/english6 and tools/english6_*.

Adapter preserves existing score/phoneme/viseme, MIDI and scheduling interfaces; it appends private experimental state outside production. This prototype is dry and monophonic. Seven tract scales were tested for safety; full seven identities, styles, harmony and expressive compatibility are NOT accepted. No failed output-weight/glide experiments replayed.

Full-velocity forward/reverse consonant/vowel transitions: 6,709,248 frames, seven tract scales, MIDI 48/60/72/84, zero nonfinite/stop failures and zero samples above .94. The predeclared raw-peak margin is .90. MIDI 48–72 peak .423475623 passes that margin. Extended MIDI 84 peaks .928018570 (voice 2) and .90495 (voice 4), so broad-register headroom FAILS. Raw output has no final limiter; do not conceal the extended-register failure.

## Decision and exact next step
NOT PROMOTED: English recognition did not improve, extended-register margin fails, host median cost increased, human listening and device timing absent. Preserve this snapshot and baseline. Do not run another open-ended gain/formant/smoothing/model loop or integrate the prototype. First collect blinded human results for these frozen clips and examine the confusion matrix; only a specific perceptual finding can justify another bounded architecture experiment. If human recognition remains poor, reassess source/filter suitability versus an independently licensed English diphone/residual approach with an explicit FM-1 memory/CPU budget, without copying a voicebank or restarting completed product systems. All original release gates remain open; no firmware/site/installer publication.
